<h3>King of Gluttony</h3>
<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/King of Gluttony.jpeg" width="200" height="300">
</p>

***Plot :*** ***She's his greatest rival... and his greatest weakness.*** 
**Handsome, talented and beloved by (almost) everyone, Sebastian Laurent is used to being on top. The heir to a culinary empire, his sharp instincts and effortless charm have made him a legend.** 

**What people don't see are the demons lurking beneath his golden-boy facade. There's only one person who's come close to knowing the real him — Maya Singh, his childhood rival and secret obsession.** 

**She's also the only one who's ever successfully challenged him. He can't stand her, but if that's true... why can't he stop thinking about her?** 

I absolutely love all Ana Huang's books, I've discovered her work through the *Twisted* series 2 and a half years ago and I've hooked on everything she writes since then. I love a good enemies-to-lovers story and I know that this one is gonna be AMAZING!
On a side note, I can't wait for her new book *The Keeper* to come out at the end of October! 


<h3>Wings of Starlight</h3>
<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/Wings of Starlight.jpeg" width="200" height="300">
</p>

***Plot :*** **Discover the sweeping, star-crossed romance between the Queen of Pixie Hollow and the Lord of the Winter Woods.**
**While most warm-season fairies fear the legends of monsters lurking in the Winter Woods, Clarion can't help being intrigued. But she has little time to dwell on daydreams while the days to her coronation dwindle away.**

**When reports of a monster crossing from Winter into Spring make their way to the to the palace, Clarion travels to the edge of winter where she finds Milori, a young guardian of the Winter Woods. Together, they form an unlikely bond as they race to save their lands.**

**But as their alliance warms, they discover there is a reason a warm-season fairy and a winter fairy must not be together. And the cost could be just as deadly as the monsters that prowl the Winter Woods.**

This is my first time reading a book with a plot involving fairies but I love it so much. I've been a fan of the Tinkerbell universe for as long as I can remember and to be able to read about the love story of Clarion and Milori that we see a glimpse of in the movie *Tinkerbell and the secret of the wings* is wonderful. I know beforehand that they won't end up together at the end of the book so I'm preparing myself to bawl my eyes out while reading the last chapters, but at least they do end up together in *The secret of the Wings* so all is fine.
